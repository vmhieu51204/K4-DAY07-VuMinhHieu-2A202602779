# VinUni Tuition Fee, Scholarship, and Financial Aid FAQs

## 1. How much does it cost to study at VinUni?
- **Listed Tuition for Undergraduate Programs:**
  - Bachelor of Nursing: **349,650,000 VND** (approximately 15,000 USD) per year.
  - All other bachelor majors (Engineering, Computer Science, Business Administration, Medical Doctor): **815,850,000 VND** (approximately 35,000 USD) per year.
- **Founder Subsidy Deduction (35%):**
  - All admitted candidates are eligible for the Fund for Education Development of 35% provided by Vingroup.
  - After the 35% subsidy, remaining tuition payable is approximately **227,000,000 VND / year** for Nursing and approximately **530,000,000 VND / year** for other undergraduate programs.
  - Further deductions apply if students receive additional merit scholarships (50%, 90%, 100%, full ride) or need-based financial aid.

## 2. What are the available payment methods for tuition fees?
There are 2 payment methods available:
1. **Direct Visa/Mastercard payment:** at the Accounting and Finance Department of VinUni campus.
2. **Online bank transfer:** through the Salesforce student portal according to detailed instructions and bank accounts sent via official email.

## 3. Do students pay tuition fees per semester or per year?
- Tuition fee payment is made **twice a year (02 installments)** at the beginning of each main semester:
  - Fall Semester installment.
  - Spring Semester installment.

## 4. Can financial aid be combined with tuition subsidy?
- Yes, the 35% founder tuition subsidy is applied automatically first. Need-based financial aid and merit-based scholarships are calculated on top to reduce the net tuition fee further.
