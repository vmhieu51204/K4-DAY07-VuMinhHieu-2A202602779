# Financial Regulations and Tariff for Student (VinUniversity)

## A. Financial Regulations and Tuition Fee Tariff for Undergraduate

### I. Listed Tuition Fee
Listed tuition fee is issued according to the standard curriculum framework announced at the time of admission.
Tuition fees include training-practical fees, facilities fees, IT support, Student activities (orientation, health services, academic advisors, career counseling, student associations, clubs, sporting-cultural events, international exchange).

**Listed tuition fee by program (Unit: VND):**
- **Bachelor of Nursing (4 years):** 349,650,000 VND / academic year; 174,825,000 VND / semester; 9,780,000 VND / credit.
- **Doctor of Medicine (6 years):** 815,850,000 VND / academic year; 407,925,000 VND / semester; 27,195,000 VND / credit.
- **Other Bachelor Programs (4 years):** 815,850,000 VND / academic year; 407,925,000 VND / semester; 27,195,000 VND / credit.

**Tuition fee listed by credit applies in following cases:**
- Students taking additional credits when studying two programs at the same time (double degree).
- Students taking additional credits beyond standard curriculum framework (e.g. double concentrations or double minors).
- Students taking additional credits when studying integrated exchange programs or visiting students.
- Course retakes: 50% of the standard per-credit tuition fee.

**General notes on tuition:**
- The tuition fees do not distinguish between training methods (online, offline, or hybrid).
- Tuition fees apply for Vietnamese and International Students with the same rate.
- Payment deadline: Students pay tuition fees twice (02) per year at the beginning of the main semesters (Fall and Spring semesters).

### II. Retention Fee and Academic Administrative Fees
- **Retention Fee (Confirmation fee for Scholarship/Tuition discount/Financial Aid):** 20,000,000 VND / time / student. Deductible from tuition fees upon enrollment.
- **Tuition Deferment or Refund processing fee:** 2,000,000 VND / request.
- **Credit Transfer evaluation fee:** 2,000,000 VND / requested course.

## C. Tuition Fees, Scholarships, and Financial Aid Policies

### 1. Merit Scholarship and Educational Development Grant
- **Founding Donor Educational Development Grant:** All students successfully enrolled in VinUniversity until the year 2030 receive an Educational Development Grant equivalent to 35% discount of listed tuition fees for the entire official duration of the program.
- **Merit-based Scholarships:** Range from 50% to 100% full tuition coverage (including the 35% grant).
- Maintained throughout the program according to VinUniversity Academic and Student Affairs regulations.

### 2. Special Tuition Fee Assistances and Payment Discounts
- **Family Tuition Assistance:** 5% reduction on listed tuition fee for students having siblings, parents, spouse, or child studying concurrently at VinUniversity (applied from the second family member enrolled).
- **Alumni Tuition Assistance:** 10% reduction on listed tuition fee for VinUni alumni pursuing a second undergraduate or graduate degree.
- **Annual Upfront Payment Discount:** 5% discount on the total payable amount for students who pay tuition upfront for the entire academic year within the announced deadline.

### 2. Late Payment Penalty and Enforcement
- Late payment fee: 2,000,000 VND for payments made after the announced deadline.
- Delays exceeding 1 to 3 weeks will result in suspension from course registration and deactivation of student portal access (Canvas, Library, SIS).

### 3. Tuition Retention and Refund Policy
- **Tuition Retention:** Leave-of-absence requests submitted by the last working day of the 3rd week of the semester may retain 100% of paid tuition for up to one (01) academic year.
- **Tuition Refund upon Withdrawal:**
  - **80% refund** of actual paid tuition if student withdraws before the official start date of the semester.
  - **50% refund** of actual paid tuition if student withdraws within two weeks from the official start date of the semester.
  - **0% refund** if student withdraws after two weeks from the official start date of the semester.
