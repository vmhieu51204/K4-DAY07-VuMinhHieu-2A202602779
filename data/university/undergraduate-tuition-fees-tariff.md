# VinUni Undergraduate Tuition Fees Tariff (Academic Year 2026-2027)

At VinUniversity, every admitted student receives a founder-funded tuition subsidy, generous scholarship programs, and clear guidance for educational financial planning.

## 1. Listed Tuition Fee by Program (Currency Unit: VND)

| Program | Standard Duration | Listed Tuition / Academic Year | Listed Tuition / Semester | Listed Tuition / Credit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bachelor of Nursing** | 4 years | 349,650,000 VND | 174,825,000 VND | 9,780,000 VND |
| **Medical Doctor** | 6 years | 815,850,000 VND | 407,925,000 VND | 27,195,000 VND |
| **Other Bachelor Programs** | 4 years | 815,850,000 VND | 407,925,000 VND | 27,195,000 VND |

## 2. Founder Subsidy & Inclusions
- **Founder Subsidy (35%):** Listed tuition fees do not include the 35% tuition subsidy provided by Vingroup – the Founder of VinUniversity. This 35% tuition subsidy is automatically granted to all admitted students (both Vietnamese and international) and remains valid throughout the entire standard duration of study.
- **Included services:** Tuition fees include training-practical fees, facilities fees, IT support, and Student activities (orientation, health services, counseling psychology, academic advisors, career counseling, student associations, clubs, sporting-cultural events, international exchange, business experiences).

## 3. Cases Where Tuition Fee by Credit Applies
Tuition fees by credit are applicable in the following scenarios:
1. Students taking additional credits when studying two programs at the same time (double degree) at VinUniversity.
2. Students taking additional credits beyond the standard curriculum framework (such as pursuing two majors / double concentrations, or two minors).
3. Students taking additional credits in an integrated exchange program at VinUni partner universities.
4. Students retaking a course due to failure to meet minimum passing grade, ineligibility for final exam, or voluntary re-registration to improve grades.
5. Conditional credit transfer students requiring supplementary courses (practice/experiment).
